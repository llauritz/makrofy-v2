What Yaffle is

Yaffle is a food diary for calories and macronutrients (protein, fat and carbohydrates). You log what you eat, set a daily goal, and see how each day compares to it. It runs in your browser and can be installed on your phone's home screen. Your log syncs across devices when you sign in with Google.

Logging food, setting goals, statistics and exporting your data are free on every plan.

AI fill

Instead of typing in the numbers yourself, you can describe what you ate, for example "two eggs and a slice of toast". AI fill searches online with Google and fills in the calories and macros for you. You can check and change every number before it's saved.

Each plan comes with a number of AI fills. Paid plans count them per 30-day cycle, which starts on the day you subscribe.

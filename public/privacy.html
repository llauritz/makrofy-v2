Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version. We store your food log so Yaffle can work and sync. When you use AI fill, the food description you type is sent to Google's Gemini to estimate calories and macros. We don't run ads, we don't use analytics trackers, and we don't sell your data. You can export your data at any time and ask us to delete it.

The controller of your personal data is Lauritz Baier (trading as "Orrei Studio"), a sole trader of Concepción Arenal 2989, Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires, Argentina ("we", "us").

For any privacy question or request, email lauritzbaier@gmail.com.

3. Data stored on your device

Yaffle stores some data in your browser so it works offline and opens instantly: a copy of your recent food log and settings (in IndexedDB and local storage), your theme and language choices, and whether you have dismissed certain tips. This storage is necessary for the app to work, and it stays on your device. Yaffle itself does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Google sign-in and Google reCAPTCHA may set their own cookies when you use them, under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the following, and only as needed:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): hosts the app, signs you in (Firebase Authentication), stores your data (Cloud Firestore), protects against abuse (App Check, reCAPTCHA Enterprise), and runs AI fill (Gemini models on Vertex AI, called from our server on Google Cloud Functions, with grounding in Google Search). Google acts as our processor under its data processing terms. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google does not use the food descriptions we send to train its models.
  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited, UK): our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle processes your payment details, billing address and tax information to sell you the subscription, and is an independent controller of that data under Paddle's Privacy Policy. We pass Paddle your Yaffle account ID so your purchase can be linked to your account.
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, where needed for tax, accounting or legal matters.
  • Authorities, such as tax authorities, courts or regulators, where the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if the Yaffle business is ever transferred. We would tell you first.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

5. International transfers

We are based in Argentina. Google stores and processes data in the United States and other countries. Paddle is based in the United Kingdom. Argentina is recognised by the European Commission as providing adequate data protection. Where your data moves to a country without such recognition, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as built into our providers' terms.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account and food log: for as long as you use Yaffle. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days; copies in Google's backups expire on Google's schedule.
  • Guest data: until you clear it or ask us to delete it.
  • AI fill requests: our server passes the request to Google and doesn't store it or log it; we keep only the food entry you save. Google may keep request logs for a limited time for abuse monitoring under its terms.
  • Usage counters: until your account is deleted.
  • Purchase and tax records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires, up to 10 years.
  • Security logs: for the periods set by Google, typically up to 30 days.
  • AI fill error reports: 90 days.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectify data that is wrong (you can edit most of it in the app);
  • erase your data;
  • restrict how we process it;
  • data portability: you can export your food log from Settings at any time, or ask us for a copy;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent, without affecting earlier processing;
  • complain to a data protection authority. In Argentina this is the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP). In the EU or UK you can complain to the authority where you live or work.

To use any of these rights, email lauritzbaier@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may need to confirm the request comes from you, for example by asking you to write from the email linked to your Google sign-in.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have similar rights to know, correct and delete your data. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), database rules that let only you read and write your own data, and App Check to block requests that don't come from the real app. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you and the relevant authorities without undue delay if a breach puts your data at risk.

9. Children

Yaffle is not meant for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

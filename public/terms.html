3. What Yaffle does

Yaffle lets you log what you eat, track calories and macronutrients (protein, fat and carbohydrates) against a daily goal, and look back at your history. You can use it as a guest without an account, or sign in with Google to back up and sync your data across devices.

Yaffle can estimate the calories and macros of a food from a short description, using artificial intelligence ("AI fill"). The free plan includes a limited number of AI fills in each 30-day cycle. Paid plans include more.

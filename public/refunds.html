Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version. If Yaffle isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of any payment and you'll get your money back, as long as you've used fewer than 100 AI fills since that payment. No reason needed.

Who handles your payment

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders, so refunds are issued by Paddle to your original payment method. Paddle's receipts and emails show our business name, Orrei Studio.

Refund window

You can request a full refund within 14 days of any payment for a Yaffle subscription. This applies to your first payment and to each renewal, on both monthly and yearly plans. You don't need to give a reason.

Because every AI fill has a real cost to us, this applies as long as you have used fewer than 100 AI fills since the payment you want refunded. If you've used more, contact us anyway: we'll look at each case fairly, for example if the service didn't work as described.

After that window, payments are generally not refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future renewals. If something went wrong, for example you were charged twice or couldn't use what you paid for, contact us anyway and we'll make it right. Your rights under consumer law in your country are not affected.

How to request a refund

  • Go to paddle.net and look up your order with the email address you paid with, or reply to your Paddle receipt email; or
  • email us at lauritzbaier@gmail.com with the email address you paid with, and we'll arrange it with Paddle.

Refunds usually reach your account within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

What happens after a refund

When a payment is refunded, the subscription it paid for ends and your account moves to the free plan. Your food log and settings stay as they are.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep your paid plan until the end of the period you've paid for. You can cancel from the subscription management link in Yaffle or in your Paddle receipt email.

See also Paddle's refund policy and our Terms of Service.

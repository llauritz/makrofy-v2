## Variants

| Variant | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `today` | The app today: one Gemini call with Google Search, the model decides whether to search (src/lib/macro-fill.ts SYSTEM_PROMPT). |
| `no-search` | Today's prompt with no search tool. The floor on cost, and the accuracy of Gemini's own knowledge. |
| `cascade` | Two-step fill from functions/src/ai-prompt.ts: ROUTING_PROMPT without search answers or hands over; SEARCH_PROMPT with search runs only after a hand-over. |
| `table-jev` | Our USDA food table first: Jev picks the food and portion and code does the sums; a miss (no match, "none", Jev unsure, not a single food) falls back to the cascade. |
| `jev-table` | Full hybrid: Jev routes; brands go to SEARCH_PROMPT with search, staples try the food table first, and everything else (and table misses) goes through the cascade. |
| `cascade:model=gemini-3.1-flash-lite` | Two-step fill from functions/src/ai-prompt.ts: ROUTING_PROMPT without search answers or hands over; SEARCH_PROMPT with search runs only after a hand-over. |
| `table-jev:model=gemini-3.1-flash-lite` | Our USDA food table first: Jev picks the food and portion and code does the sums; a miss (no match, "none", Jev unsure, not a single food) falls back to the cascade. |

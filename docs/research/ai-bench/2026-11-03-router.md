# AI fill router bench — 2026-11-03 — Gemini routing vs Jev routing

_Run on the owner's Mac with `node scripts/ai-bench/run.mjs --rounds 3`: 36 foods × 3 rounds, gemini-3.5-flash-lite at LOW on the Vertex `global` endpoint, Jev `jev-latest` (resolved to jev-1.14.0). Every design is built from the same raw calls, so the differences come from the routing alone. Raw calls: `runs/2026-11-03T14-33-17-router.json`. The cascade arm used the bench's stand-in prompt. Re-run it once the price review's `ROUTING_PROMPT` is on main._

## Takeaways

- **Staples are where today's fill wastes money.** It searched on 72% of staple fills. Every design with a routing step brings staples down to $0.5 per 1,000 fills with no loss in accuracy (48/48 within tolerance).
- **Jev's three-way choice routes perfectly on search.** Of 108 decisions, it never left a brand unsearched and never wasted a search. Its one mix-up was calling vague dishes "staple" (16 times, 6 of them defensible). That did no harm, because the fill without search asks the question itself.
- **Jev router, then fill** is the best design here: 75/75 kcal within tolerance, $7.38 per 1,000 fills (today $14.79), median 1.95 s (today 2.67 s). A Gemini router call is just as accurate but 1.5 s slower (1.25 s per call against 0.22 s for Jev, p90 0.31 s).
- **The cascade (stand-in prompt) is the cheapest at $6.11, but it's less accurate.** It answered Snickers from memory (280 kcal, then asked twice), and its prompt made it ask a question about "an apple" twice. It's faster on staples (1.47 s against 1.86 s) and slower on brands (4.15 s against 3.26 s).
- **Jev as a checker doesn't work.** It rated nearly every answer made without search as plausible (median 0.75, lowest 0.45), including Chobani at 130 kcal (label 110) and the Havanna alfajor at 230 (about 206). At a 0.5 threshold it almost never searches. At 0.8 it searches at random. This matches the overconfidence reported elsewhere.
- **Jev's yes/no "needs a search" is poor.** It made the right call on only 69% of foods, missing 9 brands and wasting 24 searches. Use the choice question, not the yes/no one.
- **Gemini without search knows many brands.** Coca-Cola, Oreo, Nutella, KFC, Haribo and the Big Mac were all within tolerance with no search. It got Chobani (130) and Havanna (230) wrong. A shared cache or Open Food Facts would cover those cases more cheaply than searching every brand.
- **Imperial units still leak** into `servingText`: 9 times in today's design.
- Search here averaged 1.02 queries per searched fill, at most 11.
